Hobbies

My hobbies are anything arts related. I love painting, sketching, and charcoal rendering, because they let me express feelings and ideas that are hard to say in words.

Sketching is the hobby I do most often. I carry a small sketchbook and draw whatever catches my eye, such as faces, plants, and everyday objects.

I also enjoy dance and singing, or anything that needs an audience. Performing helps me build confidence, and I like sharing joy with the people watching.

As a BSIT student at Caraga State University, I study Web Design, Programming, Networking, and Troubleshooting. Web design is my favorite subject because it lets me turn my love of art into something people can see on a screen.

I use my art skills when I choose colors, layouts, and fonts for my web pages. A good design makes a page easier to read and more enjoyable to use.

In my free time, I practice new art techniques and try to improve a little each day. I hope to combine art and technology in my future career.
